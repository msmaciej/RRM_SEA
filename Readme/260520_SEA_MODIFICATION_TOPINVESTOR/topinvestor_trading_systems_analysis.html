OXO indicator and scanner

The OXO-Indicator is a proprietary reversal-point detector compiled as .ex4 (MT4 binary — source code not available). It paints arrows on the chart marking probable turning points. Key properties from the PDFs:

  • Does not repaint — once a signal arrow appears on a closed candle, it stays.
  • Does not show direction — it marks reversal zones. Direction must come from context (trend, EMA, higher TF).
  • Best in strong trends during high-liquidity sessions (London/NY open ± 1.5h).
  • The OXO-Scanner scans all pairs/timeframes and shows a dashboard of recent signals (configurable lookback: 1–3 candles).

The OXO.tpl template adds EMA 50 (dashed) and EMA 200 (solid purple) as the trend-context overlays alongside the OXO indicator.

The three named systems

System 1 EMA bounce with OXO

Price pulls back to EMA 50 or EMA 200 in a strong trend → OXO signal appears at/near the moving average → enter on next candle open. SL below the MA (or below second swing). Works best after Golden Cross (EMA50 × EMA200). The author emphasizes retest-and-bounce — price pierces then returns to the MA and gets rejected.

Indicators: EMA 50, EMA 200, OXO reversal signal  |  Exit: EMA 9 trailing close (or fixed TP)
System 2 Signal at key level

OXO signal appears at a well-respected horizontal support/resistance level (double/triple top or bottom, prior swing highs/lows). Must be in a strong trend. The level should have been respected multiple times from both sides.

Indicators: S/R levels, OXO signal  |  Exit: EMA 9 trail or TP at next level
System 3 "Samobój" (standalone OXO)

OXO used alone — the author explicitly discourages this but documents the strict conditions: strong impulse followed by clearly weakening correction, signal candle must close above 75% of its body (shows strength), ideally with divergence on an oscillator, and near a MA or level. Essentially a "last resort" mode requiring extreme selectivity.

Indicators: OXO alone + price action quality filter  |  Exit: EMA 9 trail

PDF 02 advanced concepts: zones, breakers, confluence (K-score)

Extreme zones

Drawn on the last candle before a strong move. Types: extreme candle body, candle with long wick, opposite-color candle before impulse. Strongest when combined with EMA 200/50.

Breakers (type 1 + 2)

Type 1: zone at last correction end that broke prior highs (stronger). Type 2: zone that started a correction (weaker). Frequently marks correction endpoints in strong trends.

Confluence K-score (the modern system)

Each tool/level present at the signal gets 1 point (EMA 200 gets 2). K-3 is minimum playable, K-4 is good, K-5+ is excellent.

Eligible elements: EMA 50 (1pt), EMA 200 (2pts), Fibonacci 0.38/0.5 retracement, S/R level, extreme zone, breaker zone, flag/pennant structure, ABC correction, OXO signal, divergence, round number level (e.g. 200.00), doji/spinning top, Wyckoff structure, volume signals.

Can we code these in SimpleEA? Mapping to PRESET_RRM

Best match

System 1 (EMA bounce) maps almost perfectly to SimpleEA's PRESET_RRM with STRAT_4EMA_LAYER. The 4-EMA phase system (TM/EM/UNO) replicates the "strong trend" filter, and Layers L1/L2/L3 detect pullback-to-EMA and recovery — exactly what System 1 does manually.
