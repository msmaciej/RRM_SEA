THE NAKED-CHART DOCTRINE
Price is the only indicator. Stochastic / RSI / MACD are dropped: a market is never so oversold it can't get more oversold.
horizontal S/R lines trend line (sparingly) fibo 0.5 / 0.618 / 0.764 (≥ H1) EMA 21 (D1) · EMA 144 / 377 (H1)
Fibo and trend lines only strengthen a level — you enter on the S/R level, never on the fibo. EMAs are dynamic levels and a slope gauge, never a crossover trigger. Pick 2–3 setups, drill them, don't switch.
MARKET STRUCTURE — SWINGS & THE 1-2-3 REVERSAL
Trend = sequence of swings. A trend change is confirmed only when price breaks the last swing low (down) / swing high (up); the position is taken on the correction back to the broken level. A range is confirmed when LH and HL form inside the previous extremes — no trade until it resolves.
S/R LEVELS — THE CORE OF THE METHOD
What makes a level tradeable: held price ≥ 2–3× dual-function (was S and R) old & long-tested > fresh not pierced too often fibo / trend-line confluence round 00 levels
Third attack on a level with a weakening trend → fade it with a small stop above the double/triple top.

SIGNAL → ENTRY → MANAGEMENT (how it would run inside SEA)
STOP LOSS · TAKE PROFIT · TRADE MANAGEMENT
SL sizing: the width of the S/R zone (5–30 pips) — the level defines the stop. Pin bar: beyond the wick, or 60 % of the bar (aggressive). Inside bar: 50 % of the mother bar (or just beyond it).
Targets: short-term S/R → 40–50 pips (SL 10–15); long-term S/R → 100+ pips; measured moves from patterns; next S/R level is always the reference.
Management: at +20 pips move SL to BE; take a partial at +20 (short-term) or +40 (long-term) and let the rest run to the next level; small TF / high volatility → take profits fast. Never let a profit turn into a loss.
Anty-Stop: stopped out in a valid trend? re-place the same pending order at the original entry; if price runs 25 pips past the stop, place it at the stop price instead.
MONEY MANAGEMENT & THE SEVEN RULES
Be the casino, not the gambler. Risk ≤ 3 % per trade (2 % vs 10 % worked example: 19 losses in a row = −30 % vs −85 %). R:R 2:1 keeps a 50 % hit-rate profitable. Scale risk down with trade frequency, small discipline, small deposit or a hostile market.
1No counter-trend after a big news move
2SL to BE at +20 pips
3A move never lasts forever
4Always wait for the good setup
5Don't enter a setup that has already played out
6Never let a profit become a loss
7Never trade out of boredom — be patient
Daily plan: morning review, pending orders on the best S/R levels, alarms a dozen pips away. Enter when the market is ready, not when you are.
